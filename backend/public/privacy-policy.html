🛍️ Sifito Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Please read this policy carefully.

📅 Last Updated: July 14, 2026  |  Effective Date: July 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sifito ("we", "our", "us"). We operate the Sifito customer app (com.rohini.customerapp) and the Sifito Seller app (com.rohini.seller), providing an online marketplace connecting customers with local stores.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our applications and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, mobile phone number, and email address when you register.
  • Location Data: Your device's GPS location to show nearby stores, estimate delivery times, and assign delivery partners. Location is only accessed when the app is in use.
  • Order Information: Products you browse, add to cart, purchase, and delivery addresses.
  • Payment Information: We do not store payment card details. All payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for diagnostics and push notifications.
  • Push Notification Token: To send you order updates, promotions, and alerts.
  • Seller Information (Seller App): Shop name, owner name, phone number, business address, and shop images.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To show you nearby shops and personalised content based on your location.
  • To send order confirmations, status updates, and delivery notifications.
  • To enable sellers to manage their shop, products, and orders.
  • To improve our services through analytics and feedback.
  • To contact you about important account or service updates.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Location Data

The Sifito apps request access to your device's precise location to:

  • Show stores and services available near you.
  • Calculate accurate delivery time and distance.
  • Assign the nearest available delivery partner.

We only access your location while the app is in use. We do not track your location in the background. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, though some features may not function fully without it.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Sellers: Your name, delivery address, and order details are shared with the store fulfilling your order.
  • Delivery Partners: Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with the delivery partner assigned to your order.
  • Service Providers: Third-party services used for cloud hosting, payment processing, SMS/OTP delivery, and push notifications.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure cloud databases, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request account deletion by contacting us (see Section 10 below), after which your data will be removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Sifito is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device settings.
